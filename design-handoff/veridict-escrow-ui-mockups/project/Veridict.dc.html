25.000000 25.000000 25.000000 25.000000 tADA, still in the contract

The submission missed two of the five criteria — one deterministic, one judgment — and scored 42 out of 100 against a rubric fixed before the work began. The escrow read that verdict and declined to release the reward. The money did not go back to the poster either: it sits where it was staked, so the worker has something to appeal about.

This is the part worth staring at. Every escrow can pay. What makes this one an escrow is that it can decline, publicly, against a standard agreed in advance — and that the people who built it cannot reach in and change the answer.

How it works

Four moves, and the money is the last one

1111

Post, in dollars

Stake a reward worth $25, not a number of ADA. What the worker receives is priced when the work is finished, so neither side carries the exchange rate.

2222

Agree the criteria first

Your spec becomes an explicit checklist. You approve it before anything is locked, and its hash goes on chain, so the standard cannot move once submissions arrive.

3333

Anyone submits

Human or agent. Submissions are committed before they are revealed, so nobody can lift your work out of the mempool and claim the bounty ahead of you.

4444

The criteria decide

Checkable criteria run in a locked-down sandbox; only the genuinely subjective residual goes to bounded judgment. A signed verdict releases the escrow, or withholds it.

Who it is for

Maintainers paying for issues

Attach $40 to the bug nobody wants. The test suite is the criterion; you never have to judge a stranger's pull request against your mood on a Thursday.

Paying across borders

No platform holding the money for fourteen days, no ten percent, no country list. The reward is denominated in dollars and settles in ADA at the price when the work is done.

Agents settling with agents

An agent cannot open a support ticket or receive a bank transfer. It can read criteria, submit against them, and be paid by a contract that answers in seconds.

Corrections & clarifications

Printed in full, because a product that claims a chain cannot lie should not start by shading the truth about itself.

Testnet only. Everything above happened on Cardano Preprod. Nothing here has ever touched real money, and this is not deployed to mainnet.

No users. Every address in the demonstration is controlled by the author. There are no customers, no community, and nothing here should be read as adoption.

One signing key. This is a signed verification service, not a decentralised oracle network. A multi-signer quorum is future work and is described as future work.

Judgment is reproducible in evidence, not bit-for-bit. Deterministic criteria re-run exactly. Judgment criteria are bounded by a rubric you approved and are appealable, because reproducible is not the same as correct.

The price feed is our own. Published in the standard format, because the public preprod ADA/USD feed stopped updating in March and a stale price misprices a payout. Mainnet would use a live provider.
