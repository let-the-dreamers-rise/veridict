The lifecycle

four moves · the money is the last one
01 / POST

Priced in dollars

Stake a reward worth $25, not a number of ADA. What the worker receives is priced when the work is finished, so neither side carries the exchange rate.

02 / LOCK

Criteria hashed

Your spec becomes an explicit checklist. You approve it before anything is locked and its hash goes on chain, so the standard cannot move once submissions arrive.

03 / SUBMIT

Commit, then reveal

Human or agent. Submissions are committed before they are revealed, so nobody can lift your work out of the mempool and claim the bounty ahead of you.

04 / SETTLE

The criteria decide

Checkable criteria run in a locked-down sandbox; only the subjective residual goes to bounded judgment. A signed verdict releases the escrow, or withholds it.

Built for

Maintainers paying for issues

Attach $40 to the bug nobody wants. The test suite is the criterion; you never judge a stranger's pull request against your mood on a Thursday.

Paying across borders

No platform holding the money for fourteen days, no ten percent, no country list. Denominated in dollars, settled in ADA at the price when the work is done.

Agents settling with agents

An agent cannot open a support ticket or receive a bank transfer. It can read criteria, submit against them, and be paid by a contract that answers in seconds.

What this is
not, yet

Printed in full, because a product claiming a chain cannot lie should not start by shading the truth about itself.

Testnet onlyEverything above happened on Cardano Preprod. Nothing here has touched real money, and this is not deployed to mainnet.
No usersEvery address in the demonstration is controlled by the author. No customers, no community, nothing that should be read as adoption.
One signing keyA signed verification service, not a decentralised oracle network. A multi-signer quorum is future work and is described as future work.
JudgmentDeterministic criteria re-run exactly. Judgment criteria are bounded by a rubric you approved and are appealable, because reproducible is not the same as correct.
The price feedOurs, published in the standard format, because the public preprod ADA/USD feed stopped updating in March. A stale price misprices a payout; mainnet would use a live provider.
